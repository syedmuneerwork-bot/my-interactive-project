↗ Resultant Vector Magnitude

Calculate the magnitude of the sum of two vectors.

Formula: R = √(Rx² + Ry²)
Rx = Ax + Bx
Ry = Ay + By

↗ Resultant Vector Direction

Calculate the direction of the resultant vector from the positive X-axis.

Formula: θ = atan2(Ry, Rx)

🎯 Projectile Motion

Assumes the projectile lands at its launch height, with no air resistance. Gravity = 9.81 m/s².

Time of Flight

Formula: T = 2u sin(θ) / g

Maximum Height

Formula: H = u² sin²(θ) / 2g

Horizontal Range

Formula: R = u² sin(2θ) / g

🔄 Centripetal Acceleration

Formula: a = v² / r

🔄 Centripetal Force

Formula: F = mv² / r
